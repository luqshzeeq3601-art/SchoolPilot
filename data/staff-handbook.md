# Sri Cempaka International Academy — Staff Operational Handbook

## 1. School Overview & Governance
### 1.1 Vision & Mission
Sri Cempaka International Academy (SCIA) is dedicated to fostering holistic academic excellence, character integrity, and global citizenship. All faculty and administrative personnel are required to embody these values in both conduct and pedagogical instruction.

### 1.2 Administrative Hierarchy
- **Board of Governors**: Strategic direction and high-level governance.
- **Principal**: Executive head of academic, compliance, and institutional operations.
- **Head of Operations (School Administrator)**: Oversees non-academic staff, facilities, procurement, regulatory compliance, and central human resource workflows.
- **Heads of Department (HoD)**: Supervise subject curriculum, teacher performance, departmental schedules, and first-level leave approvals.
- **Teaching Faculty**: Responsible for syllabus delivery, student assessment, co-curricular advisory, and pastoral care.

## 2. Working Hours & Attendance Protocols
### 2.1 Daily Timetable
- Standard operational hours for academic staff: **07:15 to 16:00** (Monday to Thursday), and **07:15 to 15:30** (Friday).
- Staff are required to clock in using the digital biometric system at the administrative foyer before **07:20**.
- Morning assembly commences promptly at **07:30**.

### 2.2 Late Arrival & Unscheduled Absences
- Staff arriving after **07:25** must register with the Administration Office.
- Three (3) unexcused late occurrences within a rolling 30-day window trigger a formal discussion with the Head of Department.
- Unnotified absence exceeding 24 hours constitutes abandonment of duty under Section 4.2 of the Employment Contract.

---

## 3. Leave Policies & Application Standard Operating Procedures
### 3.1 Annual Leave (Administrative & Term-Time Rules)
- Teaching staff do not take discretionary annual leave during active academic terms (Weeks 1 through 10 of each term).
- Annual leave must be taken during designated term breaks, scheduled via the SchoolPilot portal at least two (2) weeks prior to term completion.

### 3.2 Medical & Sick Leave
- Full-time staff are entitled to **14 days** of paid sick leave per calendar year (increased to 18 days for staff with >2 years tenure).
- Any sick leave claim must be supported by a certified Medical Certificate (MC) issued by a registered Malaysian Medical Council (MMC) clinic or hospital.
- MCs must be uploaded to the SchoolPilot portal within **24 hours** of resumption of duty. Digital scans or clear photographs are accepted.
- Traditional or non-certified practitioner notes will not qualify for paid medical leave.

### 3.3 Emergency Leave (Cuti Kecemasan)
- Entitlement: Up to **3 working days** per calendar year, deducted from the overall leave allocation or converted to unpaid leave upon exhaustion.
- **Procedure**:
  1. The teacher must notify their Head of Department (HoD) and the Administration Office via the SchoolPilot platform or direct phone call before **06:45** on the morning of the absence.
  2. The teacher must prepare and transmit emergency lesson relief work (e-relief tasks) for all scheduled teaching periods to the departmental group.
  3. A formal leave request must be submitted via SchoolPilot within **24 hours** detailing the nature of the emergency.
  4. Approval chain: HoD verification followed by final sign-off by the School Administrator.

### 3.4 Compassionate Leave & Maternity / Paternity Leave
- **Compassionate Leave**: Granted up to **3 consecutive days** upon the death of an immediate family member (parent, spouse, child, sibling).
- **Maternity Leave**: **98 consecutive calendar days** in accordance with the Malaysian Employment Act. Notification must be submitted 60 days before the anticipated confinement date.
- **Paternity Leave**: **7 consecutive calendar days** for registered male employees for up to five (5) surviving children.

## 4. Relief Teaching & Class Substitution Protocols
### 4.1 Internal Relief Cover (Guru Ganti)
- When a teacher is absent, the HoD is responsible for assigning relief periods from available non-contact teachers.
- Relief assignments are published on the digital noticeboard by **07:15** daily.
- Teachers on relief duty must actively supervise student tasks and maintain discipline inside the assigned classroom. Sitting outside or assigning passive study without supervision is strictly prohibited.

---

## 5. Examination & Assessment Procedures
### 5.1 Examination Paper Security
- All mid-year and end-of-year examination papers must be drafted, peer-reviewed, and submitted to the HoD four (4) weeks before examination week.
- Printing of examination booklets must occur strictly in the High-Security Reprographics Room. No soft copies may be stored on unsecured USB drives or public cloud storage.

### 5.2 Invigilation Protocols
- Invigilators must report to the Examination Hall twenty (20) minutes prior to paper commencement.
- Mobile phones, smart watches, and laptops must be deposited in the secure locker during invigilation.
- Any suspected academic malpractice must be documented on Form EX-3 and reported immediately to the Chief Invigilator.

---

## 6. Student Safeguarding & Safety Regulations
### 6.1 Child Protection Code
- SCIA maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards physical punishment, emotional belittling, or inappropriate communication.
- Staff must never be alone with a student behind closed, opaque doors. Always maintain line-of-sight visibility.
- Any safeguarding concern or disclosure must be escalated to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) within **two (2) hours**.

### 6.2 Campus Evacuation & Fire Drill
- Fire alarm signal: Continuous ringing siren.
- Staff must immediately guide students via primary evacuation stairs to Assembly Area B (Primary Football Field).
- Homeroom teachers must carry the physical attendance register and report student headcounts within six (6) minutes.

---

## 7. IT & Information Security Guidelines
### 7.1 Acceptable Use of Institutional Technology
- Official school communications with parents and students must use official `@cempaka.edu.my` accounts. Personal WhatsApp or social media direct messages with students are prohibited.
- Educational data, student grades, and medical records are classified as Confidential under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
- Staff leaving their workstations must lock screens (`Win + L`).

---

## 8. Disciplinary & Grievance Procedures
### 8.1 Progressive Disciplinary Matrix
- **Stage 1**: Verbal reminder and mentoring record (kept by HoD).
- **Stage 2**: Formal written warning issued by the School Administrator.
- **Stage 3**: Domestic inquiry presided by the Board of Governors.
